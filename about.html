HERE-WARD

a quilt-along with Zak Foster

HEREWARD is a quilt-along that helps us see how we got here, so we can move forward knowing what has carried us this far.

Over 2027, one prompt at a time, we’ll sort through a collection of memories, places and objects, looking for the ones that still hum, the ones that have been quietly shaping us all along.

By the end of the year, we look back across the quilt and see the threads that run through our lives.

How it works

  • 24 prompts, one every two weeks, beginning Friday, January 1, 2027.
  • Every prompt begins with a few ideas for journaling before we sew. Writing, just like sewing, slows us down, so we notice what a quick glance might miss.
  • One quilt all year. Every prompt adds another layer to the same quilt, so it deepens as the year goes on.
  • I'll sew along with you. I'm making my own quilt one prompt at a time, in real time, right along with you.
