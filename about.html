HERE-WARD

a quilt-along for 2027

a map of the years in cloth

HEREWARD is a year of reflection, one prompt at a time. Over 2027 you write your way into your own life and sew what you find into a single whole-cloth quilt. By the end, we look back across the cloth and see the throughlines that have run through our lives all along.

How it works

  • 24 prompts, one every two weeks, beginning Friday, January 1, 2027. Every prompt explores a different part of where you come from.
  • We write before we sew. Each prompt walks you through four short gates that help you hone in on your charge, the current that makes a memory matter to you. The sewing follows.
  • One quilt. Every prompt adds to the same whole-cloth quilt, so by the end of the year you are holding a quilt made of your own story, with the threads that run through your life finally in view.
  • Zak sews along with you. There is no sample quilt, because I'll be making mine in real time, right beside you.

You are not doing it alone
